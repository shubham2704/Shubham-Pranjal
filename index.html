It Started With a Smile

"Some people search the whole world for their person. Ours just walked in with a smile that changed everything."

What began as a family introduction quickly became something neither of us expected — a friendship that turned into late-night talks, late-night talks that turned into travel plans, and travel plans that turned into "I can't imagine doing any of this without you."

She's the one who finds the hidden restaurants in every new city. He's the one who says "let's go" before she even finishes the sentence. Together, we've discovered that love isn't just about the big moments — it's the in-between ones, the food comas, and the road trips where we get happily lost.
